<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Live cut to verifier/export | On-disk files may have changed after acceptance. |
| ZIP archive to restore staging | Archive members and compressed sizes are untrusted. |
| Staging to restored cut | A verified archive may publish only into a new private destination. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T-01-09 | Tampering | CSV/SQLite/manifest | mitigate | D-07/D-08 recompute all hashes, row/key counts, integrity, FKs and relationship digest before export and after extraction. |
| T-01-10 | Elevation of privilege | ZIP member paths | mitigate | Reject traversal, absolute, symlink, duplicate and unexpected members before extraction. |
| T-01-11 | Denial of service | ZIP decompression | mitigate | Limit compressed and total uncompressed bytes and member count; test oversized archive. |
| T-01-12 | Information disclosure | Export/restore destination | mitigate | D-06/D-08 enforce private roots and safe diagnostics with no row values. |
| T-01-13 | Spoofing | Reconciled cash/return cohort claims | mitigate | D-10/D-11 revalidate observed balance, active economic-event chain and aggregate cohort links; unsupported labels fail or remain unknown. |
</threat_model>
