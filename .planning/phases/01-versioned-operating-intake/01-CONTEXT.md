# Phase 1 Context: Versioned Operating Intake

This records the recommended GSD defaults approved for planning. The proposed source names in `01-RESEARCH.md` are resolved here as the Phase 1 implementation contract. These are analytical source facts, not authority to ingest real client data during development or execute business actions.

## Decisions

### D-01 — Separate aggregate operating workspace

Create a versioned `operating-v1` path alongside the released v0.2 warehouse and v0.3 workbook path. Keep `alma/interchange.py`, `alma/warehouse.py`, `models/schema.sql`, existing evidence, and historical release manifests behaviorally unchanged. Aggregate sales do not become fabricated orders, customers, or order items. Use Python 3.11+, the standard library, SQLite 3.37+ `STRICT` tables, CSV, and JSON. No server, paid inference, or new package is needed.

### D-02 — One exact source registry and versioned pack classes

`alma/operating_contracts.py` is the sole ordered registry for the 22 CSVs below: field order, type, nullable status, unit, grain, key, FK, enum, and provenance. Every header is exact and ordered; every file is present, even when its declared coverage is `MISSING` or `NOT_APPLICABLE`. `source_ref` is the **last column exactly once** in every CSV, including composite-key tables. An initializer emits a blank pack or a visibly synthetic example pack from the registry under `client/source-packs/v1/`; only blank/synthetic content is public. `metadata.json` declares `contract_version: "operating-v1"`, `input_class` (`BLANK`, `SYNTHETIC_EXAMPLE`, or `PRIVATE`), `cutoff_at` (ISO 8601 timestamp with offset), `timezone` (IANA name matching the offset at cutoff), `currency: "MXN"`, and a `coverage` object with exactly one entry per CSV stem. A blank template may leave cutoff/timezone unset and cannot be built until completed. Example packs use fixed synthetic IDs and dates.

Each coverage entry has `status`, `window_start`, and `window_end` (ISO dates or null). Status is one of `COMPLETE`, `PARTIAL`, `ZERO`, `MISSING`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `ESTIMATED`, `ERROR`. `ZERO` means observed complete coverage with zero rows; numeric `0` remains a real observed zero inside a row. `MISSING`/`NOT_APPLICABLE` require zero rows; `ERROR` is retained in validation diagnostics but blocks publication. `PARTIAL` and `ESTIMATED` remain distinct from `COMPLETE`. Windows must be ordered and end no later than cutoff's local date. Pack metadata and rows contain no names, contact details, arbitrary prose, or customer/order identifiers.

### D-03 — Ordered v1 CSV contract

The table lists the **entire ordered header before trailing `source_ref`**. `?` marks a nullable field; all other fields are required. `_cents` are signed or nonnegative integer MXN cents as specified below; `_units`, `_minutes`, `quantity`, `quantity_basis`, and `_bps` are integers; `net_revenue_cents` and nullable `variable_cost_cents` may be signed because returns can reverse an earlier date's delivery. All other `_cents` amounts are nonnegative except D-10 opening/closing balances. Dates are ISO calendar dates. All IDs/codes are bounded non-personal tokens; `source_ref` identifies a non-personal source record or document token, never a path, name, URL, email, phone, or free text.

### D-04 — Types, enum sets, and cross-source identity

Use `^[A-Za-z0-9][A-Za-z0-9._:-]{0,63}$` for identifiers/codes and `source_ref` (no spaces, `@`, slashes, or backslashes); `recipient_ref` is an opaque token under the same rule. Define and enforce these enums in the registry: lifecycle `DRAFT|ACTIVE|RETIRED`; sales/availability coverage `COMPLETE|PARTIAL|ESTIMATED`; movement type `OPENING|RECEIPT_ACCEPTED|SALE_OUT|RETURN_RESTOCK|LOAN_OUT|LOAN_IN|ADJUSTMENT_IN|ADJUSTMENT_OUT`; reservation event `PLACE|RELEASE|CONSUME`; unmet-demand reason `STOCKOUT|SIZE_UNAVAILABLE|OTHER_RECORDED`; cost component classification `DIRECT|ALLOCATED|EXCLUDED`; `required_flag` `0|1`; cost quality `KNOWN|MISSING|ESTIMATED|NOT_APPLICABLE`; allocation method `UNITS|WEIGHT|DIRECT`; purchase status `OPEN|PARTIAL|CLOSED|CANCELLED`; obligation status `OPEN|PARTIAL|SETTLED|VOID`; cash level `RECONCILED|COMMITTED|EXPECTED|UNDATED|SCENARIO`; direction `INFLOW|OUTFLOW`; expense status `INCURRED|VOID`; quality event `RETURN_REPORTED|RETURN_RECEIVED|INSPECTED|RESTOCKED|REJECTED|REFUNDED`; readiness `READY|BLOCKED|REVIEW`; loan condition `GOOD|DAMAGED|UNKNOWN`; loan status `OPEN|PARTIAL|RETURNED|LOST`. `origin_type` is `PURCHASE_ORDER|EXPENSE` and `origin_id` must resolve to exactly one matching primary key in that table. `currency` is `MXN`. Dates must parse as real calendar dates; non-null event dates cannot exceed cutoff's local date, except promised/due dates. Boolean integer flags accept only 0/1. Money and counts use integer text without decimal/scientific notation, never binary float. Amounts other than the signed exceptions in D-03/D-10, quantities, and rates are nonnegative; `selling_fee_bps` is 0..10000. No conversion from currency values or fractional units occurs in Phase 1.

Every SKU FK resolves to `sku_catalog`; purchase receipt to purchase order; cost component to cost version; allocation to component and SKU; payment to obligation; expense/purchase order optional budget to budget; quality optional receipt to receipt; movements to their declared receipt, quality event, loan or composite sales key; budget allocations to budget and declared purchase-order/expense origin. `included_in_component_id` is a same-version component FK, cannot self-reference or form a cycle. A cost allocation must target the cost version's SKU; budget allocations must match the budget drop/channel and sum to no more than their origin cents. Enforce `restocked_units <= returned_units` (returns may exceed same-day deliveries because cohorts span dates); `reserved_units + non_sellable_units <= on_hand_units`; `received_units = inspection_units + accepted_units + rejected_units` and cumulative receipt units no greater than ordered units; applied payments no greater than original obligation; non-null cost allocations plus residuals exactly reconcile their source component cents without double allocation. `returned_date >= borrowed_date`, `due_date >= borrowed_date`, `period_start <= period_end`, and receipt date >= order date. Availability minutes satisfy `sellable_minutes + stockout_minutes <= observed_minutes <= 1440`; uncovered minutes stay unknown. Failed relationships block publication; no inferred joins or synthetic customers/orders.

### D-05 — Distinct economic and stock facts

Purchase order is commitment; receipt is physical arrival; inspection/acceptance/rejection are receipt dispositions; inventory count is observed position; quality/return and loan are separate source events. `inventory_movements` is the sole stock delta ledger: each `RECEIPT_ACCEPTED`, `RETURN_RESTOCK`, `LOAN_OUT`, `LOAN_IN` or `SALE_OUT` movement requires its matching source key, units reconcile, and that source cannot be posted twice; adjustments require independent `source_ref`. `OPENING` occurs once per SKU at the movement coverage window start. Direction derives from movement type and units remain positive. `inventory_reservations` is the sole reservation delta ledger; `PLACE|RELEASE|CONSUME` events share a stable reservation ID and cannot create a negative running balance. Active reservations reconcile to a complete observed count on the same date. `availability_daily` records variant×day exposure and `unmet_demand` records demand explicitly; an absent/partial source never implies zero exposure or zero demand. Obligation is the sole payable origin for a purchase order or expense; payment applies to an obligation once. `cash_events` with `RECONCILED` and a `payment_id` represents that observed payment; an independently observed cash inflow/outflow may instead use a unique non-personal `source_ref` without a payment link, but D-10 observed balance evidence is required for either. `COMMITTED` requires an `obligation_id`; cash events never materialize another payment or expense row. Each row has required `economic_event_id` and optional `supersedes_event_id` per D-11; only the unsuperseded active leaf of a same-identity chain is eligible for a cash layer. `SCENARIO` is never reconciled cash. A payment_id can appear on at most one active RECONCILED cash event, with matching amount, date and OUTFLOW direction. A RECONCILED cash event without payment_id must have no obligation_id and its `(scenario_id, source_ref)` must be unique. `EXPECTED`, `UNDATED`, and `SCENARIO` have neither reference. `budget_allocations` assign origins to a budget/drop/channel without creating another payable or expense. Phase 1 stores source facts and validates links; Phase 2 defines monetary, stock, exposure, and demand marts.

### D-06 — Fail-closed private build and immutable cut

Run full-pack privacy, exact header, type, coverage, duplicate/composite-key, referential, and business checks before publishing. Reject malformed, repeated header, missing/extra columns, ragged rows, unknown versions/enums, invalid dates, undeclared blanks, duplicate keys even with identical rows, conflicting IDs, non-PII token violations, and PII-like columns/values. Diagnostics include file, row, column, and code but no private cell values. Reject symlinks, traversal, and private paths resolving outside `.local/`; use an injectable disposable private root only in synthetic tests. Build a staging SQLite database with `STRICT` source tables, PK/FK/UNIQUE/CHECK, `PRAGMA foreign_keys=ON`, and `foreign_key_check`; publish only into a new absent `.local/operating-cuts/<cut_id>/` directory after all checks pass. Never overwrite an accepted cut or publish metrics on failed intake.

### D-07 — Stable identity, manifest, and unknown states

Canonical JSON uses UTF-8, sorted object keys, fixed separators, and `allow_nan=False`. `cut_id` is SHA-256 of canonical `{contract_version,input_class,cutoff_at,timezone,currency,coverage,source_sha256}` with source hash keys sorted; exclude `cut_id` and digest fields from their own preimage. Hash exact CSV bytes separately from a canonical typed-row digest. `workspace.json` records `cut_id`, contract version, input class, cutoff/timezone, coverage, quality status, source SHA-256 per file, metadata hash, normalized-row digest, SQLite SHA-256, per-table row/key counts, and relationship-check result/digest. Preserve `null`, observed zero, `PARTIAL`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `ESTIMATED`, and input `ERROR` as distinct; `ERROR` blocks a published cut. A hash is an integrity check, not a proof against a writer who can change both data and hashes.

### D-08 — Portable export, restore, and verification

Export a verified cut as a private ZIP containing original CSV bytes, `metadata.json`, `workspace.json`, and SQLite bytes. The archive is written only under `.local/operating-exports/`. Restore into a new absent private destination after rejecting unsafe ZIP members, symlinks, duplicates, unexpected files, oversized files, path escape, bad hashes, mismatched cut identity, bad row/key counts, failed SQLite integrity/FKs, or changed relationship digest. `verify` checks those invariants on a live workspace, too. A successful restore has the same source hashes, normalized digest, SQLite byte hash, row/key counts, FK result, relationship digest, and cut identity. Neither export nor restore changes source content or an existing accepted cut.

### D-09 — Compatibility and test boundary

Phase 1 tests use blank/synthetic fixtures and disposable private roots. Include positive, partial/unknown, malformed, duplicate, PII, FK, double-count, tamper, path, immutability, and export/restore controls. Run the affected unittest module and the existing full suite. Preserve existing v0.2/v0.3 commands, fixtures, generated hashes, and release behavior. No real customer data or private filled pack is committed or sent to an external service.

### D-10 — Evidence gates for cash and mature return attribution

`cash_balance_evidence` is a separate non-PII source, not another cash event. Its `scenario_id` is an opaque non-personal token, and opening/closing balances may be signed integer MXN cents. `evidence_status` is `OBSERVED|PARTIAL|MISSING|ESTIMATED|ERROR`. `OBSERVED` requires both balances and both offset timestamps, ordered consistently with `period_start` and `period_end`. A `cash_events.level=RECONCILED` row requires an `OBSERVED` evidence window for its scenario/date; every observed evidence window must reconcile `closing_balance_cents - opening_balance_cents` to the signed sum of its covered active, unsuperseded `RECONCILED` economic events exactly in cents. Overlapping observed windows for a scenario fail. If opening/closing evidence is absent, partial or estimated, no event in that window may claim `RECONCILED`; keep its cash state unknown/blocked or a truthful non-reconciled level. Never turn missing evidence into a zero balance or inferred bank account, and never store bank credentials or account numbers.

`sales_aggregates.delivery_cohort_id?` is an aggregate, non-personal delivered cohort token, unique to one sales aggregate key when present. `quality_events.delivery_cohort_id?` may link a return/quality event to that cohort and must resolve to a sales aggregate of the same SKU. For `COMPLETE` quality coverage, every return-related event that contributes to a mature return rate requires a valid cohort link; missing link makes that attribution `PARTIAL`/unknown, not zero. Keep reported return, physical receipt, inspection, refund and restock as distinct events. No individual customer/order identifier is created.

### D-11 — Stable cash economic identity and forecast replacement

Every `cash_events` row has a required bounded, non-personal `economic_event_id`. `event_id` identifies the immutable evidence row; `economic_event_id` identifies the underlying cash inflow or outflow across forecast revisions and eventual observation. `supersedes_event_id?` is a self-FK to a prior `cash_events.event_id` in the same scenario and economic identity. It cannot refer to itself, form a cycle, or fork: one predecessor has at most one direct successor and one `(scenario_id, economic_event_id)` group has exactly one active leaf. Multiple rows with the same economic identity but no valid linear supersession chain fail. A successor keeps `direction`, `currency`, and economic origin consistent; if a forecast obligation later has an observed payment, that payment must resolve to the same obligation. Amount and date may change when evidence improves. A `RECONCILED` row is terminal and cannot be superseded; `SCENARIO` rows remain hypothetical and cannot supersede or be superseded by actual/forecast layers. A forecast at `UNDATED`, `EXPECTED`, or `COMMITTED` may be replaced by a later truthful forecast or observed `RECONCILED` row, but a lower-evidence row cannot replace a higher-evidence row. Only active leaves contribute to cash layer sums and D-10 opening/closing reconciliation. The database declares the self-FK and unique non-null predecessor, while whole-pack validation rejects cycles, forks, cross-identity links, origin changes and double-count paths. The relationship digest and archive verification include the complete supersession edges, active-leaf IDs, economic identities and origin bindings; tampering with one link fails verification even if row counts and amounts stay equal.
